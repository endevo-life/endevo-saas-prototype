## 1. Design Philosophy

**Eternal Geometry** — where readiness becomes permanence, and order becomes legacy.

Eternal Geometry begins with a paradox: that the most profound way to honor what endures is to celebrate what is alive. The movement draws its visual vocabulary from the architecture of continuity — concentric forms that echo outward like ripples from a stone dropped in still water, interlocking geometries that suggest one life's order becoming another's foundation. Every composition is constructed with the painstaking precision of a master engraver working on a timepiece meant to outlast its maker.

Color operates as emotional cartography. Deep midnight blue serves as the foundational chromatic field — the infinite, breathing blue of a clear sky at the boundary between dusk and darkness. Warm champagne gold enters as luminous counterpoint: the precise warmth of candlelight on old paper, of inherited things that carry meaning beyond their material.

The philosophy demands "luminous gravity" — compositions that feel both substantial and light, grounded and elevated. Like the best memorial architecture, the work achieves emotional power through restraint, geometry, and material honesty rather than through dramatic gesture or symbolic excess.

## 3. Typography System

Typography exists at two extremes — monumental single words or whispered annotations — never in the middle register of ordinary communication. When text appears, it functions as inscription rather than information: carved, considered, permanent.

### Primary Heading: Italiana

Used for the brand name and major display text. Italiana is an elegant serif with fine strokes that evoke engraved timepieces and architectural inscriptions. Used at large sizes only (36pt+). Always uppercase for the monogram and brand name.

### Tagline & Subheadings: Jura Light/Medium

Jura provides a clean, modern geometric counterpoint to Italiana's classical elegance. Used for taglines, section labels, and navigational text. Light weight for taglines, Medium for emphasis. Letter-spacing: +2-3%.

### Body Copy: Instrument Sans

A contemporary sans-serif for all body text, descriptions, and interface copy. Regular weight for body, Bold for emphasis. Comfortable reading at 11-14pt. Line height: 1.5-1.6x.

### Data & Scores: JetBrains Mono

Monospace font for the Readiness Score, numerical data, and technical labels. Communicates precision and system-level credibility. Always in Champagne Gold on dark backgrounds.

### Type Hierarchy

## 4. Shape Language

Every visual element in Eternal Geometry is rooted in a precise geometric vocabulary. These forms are not decorative — they are structural philosophy made visible.

### Concentric Rings

The primary motif. Concentric circles radiating outward suggest continuity, growth rings, orbits, and ripples from a stone dropped in still water. Rings should fade in opacity from center to edge (100% to 15%), creating depth through translucency. Minimum 3 rings, maximum 7 for any composition.

### Clock-Face Tick Marks

12 evenly-spaced tick marks at 30 degree intervals, skipping the four cardinal points (12, 3, 6, 9 o'clock). These suggest time, precision, and the measured passage of moments. Ticks are rendered in Champagne Gold at 60-80% opacity.

### Diamond Markers

Small diamond shapes placed at the four cardinal points of the outermost ring. Each diamond represents one of the four life domains: Legal (top), Financial (right), Digital (bottom), Physical (left). Rendered as 45-degree-rotated squares in Champagne Gold.

### Golden Cross / Compass Crosshair

Subtle orthogonal lines extending through the center of the ring system. This element anchors the composition and suggests navigation, orientation, and the intersection of life domains. Rendered at very low opacity (15-25%) in Champagne Gold.

### Tapered Separators

Horizontal dividers that fade from full opacity at center to transparent at edges. Used between sections of text. Created using per-pixel opacity gradients in Champagne Gold. These replace hard rules and add elegance to layouts.

## 5. Logo System

The Legacy Readiness OS logo is a monogram-based mark built from the letters L and R, enclosed within the concentric ring system. The monogram is constructed with geometric precision — rectangles, arcs, and carefully measured proportions that give the letterforms architectural weight.

### Primary Logo
The full primary mark on ivory background. Use this as the default logo wherever possible.

### Icon Mark
Square format for app icons, favicons, and social media profiles. Transparent background. Minimum size: 32px.

### Dark Background Version
For use on Midnight Blue or dark surfaces. The concentric rings and monogram render in Champagne Gold and Pearl.

### Light Background Version
For use on white or light surfaces. Full color mark with Navy Deep as the dominant tone.

### Endevo Lockup
The full brand hierarchy including the four life domain labels and "Powered by Endevo" attribution. Use for formal presentations, partnership materials, and pitch decks.

## 6. Logo Usage Rules

### Clear Space
Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the "L" in the monogram on all sides. No other visual elements, text, or edges should intrude into this space.

### Minimum Size
Primary logo: minimum 120px wide (digital) or 1.5 inches (print). Icon mark: minimum 32px (digital) or 0.4 inches (print). Below these sizes, the concentric ring detail is lost.

### Approved Backgrounds

**Preferred:** Ivory (#FCFAF5), White (#FFFFFF), Midnight Blue (#12162A), Navy Deep (#1C2644).

**Acceptable:** Pearl (#EBE8E1), photography with sufficient contrast (test at 50% scale).

**Never:** Bright colors, busy patterns, gradients that compete with the ring system, or backgrounds below 4.5:1 contrast ratio.

### Do Not

- Rotate, skew, or distort the logo in any way.
- Change the colors of the monogram or rings independently.
- Add drop shadows, outlines, or effects to the logo.
- Place the logo inside a container or shape that competes with the ring system.
- Use the icon mark at sizes where the ring detail becomes a blur.
- Separate the monogram from the ring system — they are one unified mark.

## 7. Brand Hierarchy

Legacy Readiness OS is the consumer-facing brand. Endevo is the technology engine underneath. The relationship is always expressed as:

> **LEGACY READINESS OS**
> *Powered by Endevo*

The four life domains are always presented in consistent order and numbering:

**01 LEGAL . 02 FINANCIAL . 03 DIGITAL . 04 PHYSICAL**

Domain labels are always uppercase, numbered, and separated by centered dots. They appear below the logo in the full lockup and can be used independently in section headers and navigation.
