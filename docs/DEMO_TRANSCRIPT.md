## 👤 Employee Experience (0:30 - 2:00)

### Dashboard Overview (0:30 - 0:50)

> "This is the employee dashboard - the heart of the learning experience. Employees see their personalized learning roadmap with clear progress indicators.
>
> Notice how we display their completion percentage, completed modules, and estimated time to finish. The visual roadmap shows exactly where they are in their journey - from the initial Peace of Mind Assessment through core modules like Digital Estate Planning, End of Life Planning, and Estate Basics."

### Interactive Roadmap (0:50 - 1:10)

> "Each module shows its status: locked, available, in progress, or completed. The roadmap design makes it clear what to work on next without feeling overwhelming.
>
> Let's click into a module. Here we see the module details page with a structured learning path. Each lesson includes video content, text explanations, and guided activities. The interface is clean and distraction-free because legacy planning requires focus and privacy."

### Learning Module (1:10 - 1:30)

> "Inside a lesson, employees can watch educational videos, read supporting content, and take notes. Notice the reflection prompts that encourage thoughtful engagement - this isn't just passive content consumption.
>
> The platform tracks completion automatically, but employees control their data. Their notes and reflections are completely private - even HR cannot access them."

### Progress & Export (1:30 - 2:00)

> "The Progress page gives employees a comprehensive view of their achievements. They can see which modules they've completed, time spent learning, and their improvement from the initial assessment.
>
> Most importantly, they can export their progress summary and completion certificates at any time. This gives them portable proof of their education that lives beyond the platform."

## 🏢 HR Admin Experience (2:00 - 3:00)

### HR Dashboard (2:00 - 2:20)

> "Now let's look at the HR perspective. HR administrators see aggregate metrics that demonstrate program value without compromising employee privacy.
>
> The dashboard shows total enrollment, active learners, completion rates, and average progress. These metrics help HR prove ROI to leadership and identify opportunities to boost engagement."

### Analytics & Insights (2:20 - 2:40)

> "The analytics view provides deeper insights. HR can see completion trends over time, identify which modules are most engaging, and track how the program performs across departments.
>
> Notice what HR doesn't see: individual employee content, personal notes, or assessment responses. ENDevo is built with privacy-by-design architecture. HR sees participation and progress, not private learning details."

### Employee Management (2:40 - 3:00)

> "HR can view their employee roster, see enrollment status, and identify who might need encouragement to start or continue their journey. They can send gentle reminders or share resources, but the learning itself remains completely private and self-paced.
>
> This balance between organizational insight and individual privacy is what makes ENDevo effective as an employee benefit."

## 🎙️ Delivery Tips

### Tone & Pacing
- **Warm and professional** - This is sensitive subject matter
- **Steady pace** - Not too fast, allow visuals to breathe
- **Empathetic** - Acknowledge the emotional nature of legacy planning
- **Confident** - You're presenting a solution to a real problem

### Visual Cues
- **Pause briefly** when transitioning between roles/screens
- **Highlight with cursor** key UI elements as you mention them
- **Smooth scrolling** - No jerky movements
- **Click deliberately** - Show interactive elements work

### Key Points to Emphasize
1. **Privacy-first design** - HR sees metrics, not content
2. **Education over documentation** - Learning, not storage
3. **Progress tracking** - Both employee and organizational
4. **Real benefit** - Addresses genuine employee need
5. **B2B SaaS model** - Organizations buy for their employees

### Technical Demo Notes
- Have all three role views ready in browser tabs
- Clear browser zoom to 100% for consistent appearance
- Close unnecessary browser UI (bookmarks bar, etc.)
- Test audio levels before recording
- Use clean background or screen-only recording
